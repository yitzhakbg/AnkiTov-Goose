- WASM is a tactical tool for the PWA, NOT a strategic rewrite. Backend stays native (manages real Anki instances: rusqlite FFI, process spawning, AnkiConnect on localhost).
- EXTRACTS to WASM: fsrs_sort.rs (pure math: D/S retrieval, urgency sort), capsule_slicer.rs (pure logic: card pool slicing, spill-to-next), track_health.rs (data transformations, aggregation).
